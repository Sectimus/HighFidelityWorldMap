# Generator dependencies. The addon itself needs nothing — this is only for
# generateHFWM.py, and CI installs from here so the workflows cannot drift
# out of step with what the pipeline actually imports.

# hfwm/ocean.py does its sea masking on the pixel array: the ocean-fill
# match in ocean_mask, and the alpha erase in erase_tile.
numpy

# the region-wide ocean-colour mask into 4-connected components, so a
# false colour match (dark terrain shading, say) that isn't actually
# joined to real open water can be told apart from it and left alone.
scipy
# build_zone_masks.py reads the hand-traced zone layers from the trace PSDs.
psd-tools
